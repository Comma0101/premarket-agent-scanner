# Tim Grittani Distillation Notes

## 1. Morning Panic Dip Buy
- **Context:** A stock that has experienced a dramatic multi-day run-up of **>= 100%** over the previous 1 to 3 weeks.
- **The Panic Drop:** A severe and accelerated intraday price drop of **>= 30%** from recent highs, driven by stop-loss triggering rather than fundamental news.
- **Volume Profile:** The sell-off must be accompanied by an extreme volume spike, measured as an RVOL of **>= 5x** normal volume.
- **Timing Constraint:** The setup is strictly bounded to the first 30 minutes of the trading day. The exact Time of Day (TOD) cutoff for entries is **10:00 AM ET**.
- **Execution:** Wait for the selling momentum to exhaust and look for a bounce, aiming for a 10%-20% quick profit. Cut losses immediately if the bounce fails.

## 2. Gap and Crap
- **Context:** Often seen in heavily promoted stocks or those with weak fundamentals gapping up on retail hype.
- **The Gap:** Requires a morning gap up of **>= 10%** at the open.
- **The Crap (Fade):** The stock must fail to make new highs within the first 30 minutes of trading, leading to a steady intraday fade.
- **Execution:** Traders short into failed pushes (lower-high structures), adapting risk (e.g., 0.5R) depending on borrow costs and volatility.
