# Bao Nguyen (Modern Rock) Distillation Notes

## Core Philosophy
Bao Nguyen, operating under the alias "Modern_Rock", is a veteran day trader and co-founder of My Investing Club (MIC). His trading style heavily emphasizes risk management, pattern recognition, and exploiting market momentum, particularly on the short side of low-float stocks.

## Key Strategies & Mechanics

### 1. VWAP Boulevard
Though popularized in the broader FinTwit community by traders like @team3dstocks, "VWAP Boulevard" is a critical concept used extensively within MIC to map out institutional support and resistance.
- **Mechanical Rule:** Identify the highest-volume trading days in a stock's history (typically the top 1-3 all-time volume days).
- **Execution:** Calculate the closing VWAP (Volume-Weighted Average Price) on those specific historical days.
- **Application:** Draw static horizontal lines at these VWAP price levels on the current intraday chart. These act as major structural support or resistance areas. If a stock approaches "VWAP Boulevard", traders expect a reaction (rejection or bounce).

### 2. "Nail and Bail" Scalping
- **Definition:** A high-speed, momentum-based scalping technique.
- **Mechanics:** Enter a trade rapidly as momentum accelerates (nailing the move) and exit swiftly for a profit before the momentum fades (bailing).
- **Requirements:** Relies heavily on Level 2 data, tape reading, and order flow rather than just daily chart patterns.

### 3. "Line to Line" Trading
- **Concept:** Range-bound trading tactic.
- **Mechanics:** Short the stock at defined resistance lines (e.g., VWAP Boulevard, standard VWAP, or premarket highs) and cover at defined support lines.
- **Discipline:** Requires strict adherence to stop-losses if the line breaks.

### 4. Fading Extended Low-Float Stocks
- **Setup:** Identifying low-float stocks that have experienced massive, unsustainable run-ups (supernovas).
- **Trigger:** Waiting for the stock to become severely extended away from its intraday VWAP.
- **Action:** Shorting the backside of the move, targeting a reversion to the VWAP.

## Risk Management & Mindset
- **Sizing Down:** Adjusting position sizes smaller to accommodate the higher volatility and wider price ranges seen in modern markets.
- **Daily Loss Limits:** Enforcing strict max daily losses to prevent account blowups.
- **A++ Setups:** Grading setups based on probability and risk-to-reward. Only taking trades that meet the highest criteria (A++), rather than forcing mediocre setups.
